# World: **StillPoint Universe**
**Genre:** Solarpunk

The StillPoint Universe traces humankind's passage from near-future upheaval to a luminous, presence-centred civilisation. The turning point is **StillPoint** technology—grass-roots, open-hardware devices that co-regulate nervous systems, slow dialogue, and seed Commons dedicated to mutual aid and contemplative engineering.

---

**Key Technologies:**

* **StillPoint devices (SP-0 → SP-Ω)** – air-gapped light/sound/haptic "co-regulators" for individuals and crowds.
* **KAIROS slow-time AI** – firmware in every StillPoint & Pebble that inserts a two-second pause before responding.
* **Pebble (presence assistant)** – palm-sized salt-crystal companion that filters digital noise by resonance with its keeper's wellbeing.
* **Memory Loom** – distributed ledger-AI braiding emotional truth with factual record; backbone of Era 3 governance.
* **Living-Light biotech** – bioluminescent fungi & plants illuminating Commons while nurturing subterranean ecologies.
* **Micro-StillPoint harmonica** – pocket co-regulator carried by couriers for roadside breath-sync.

---

**Social Structures:**

* **Resting Places & Patchwork Commons** – self-organising hubs practising StillFlow rituals and mutual aid.
* **Data-Aligned Federations** – metric-driven collectives favouring quantified governance and large-scale optimisation.
* **Courier Guild** – oath-bound couriers who hand-carry data shards and memory stones to uphold embodied trust.
* **Right of Reflection** – globally protected daily hour reserved for contemplative practice.

---

**Character Archetypes:**

* **StillPoint Tinker** – DIY engineer iterating devices and teaching repair (e.g., Gray Tesluk).
* **Sanctuary Mentor** – elder who embodies living StillPoint presence (e.g., Elder Maren).
* **Courier Wayfarer** – roving witness transporting firmware shards across bioregions (e.g., Isha "North-Run" Calder).
* **Perennial Scholar-Guide** – synthesises science & mysticism for public teaching (e.g., Lorai).
* **Grief-Forged Inventor** – creates breakthrough tech from loss, becomes reluctant elder (e.g., Sajan).

---

**Cultures:**

* **Presence-First Commons** – value co-regulation, open logbooks, and shared tech stewardship.
* **Federation Citizens** – prioritise data efficacy, competitive innovation, and macro-metrics.
* **Courier Brotherhood/Sisterhood** – honour silence, wax seals, and story-circles around communal fires.

---

**Environment:**

* **Climate:** temperate-shifted; harsher extremes buffered by local resilience tech.
* **Geography:** repurposed megachurch sanctuaries, underground salt vaults, mountain retreats, prairie solar-rail lines.
* **Notable Locations:**

  * *Minneapolis Resting Place* – first communal StillPoint sanctuary in an abandoned megachurch.
  * *Hawthorn Patchwork Commons* – constellation of micro-hubs and container workshops linked by bioluminescent footpaths.
  * *Manitou Salt Commons* – subterranean haven where the Pebble and Courier Guild were born.
  * *Mountain Sanctuary* – mist-clad retreat whose cave murals depict ancient presence rituals.
  * *Prairie Presence Corridor* – 1 800 km e-moto route with breath-work border stops.

---

**Plot Seeds:**

* A StillPoint Tinker discovers their device is being reverse-engineered by a corporation and must decide whether to release the blueprints freely or protect them.
* A Courier Wayfarer carrying critical Memory Loom data gets stranded during a storm and must trust a Federation enclave that views their mission with suspicion.
* A Sanctuary Mentor guides a grief-stricken inventor through creating their first StillPoint device, helping them transform loss into community healing.
* Two neighboring Commons with different approaches to technology must unite when their shared water system fails, testing their ability to find stillness amid crisis.
* A Scholar-Guide must convince a Data-Aligned Federation to adopt the Right of Reflection when their efficiency metrics show decreased productivity during contemplative hours.

---

**Themes:**

* Presence over distraction
* Grief transmuted into communal creativity
* Radical transparency & DIY tech ethics
* Voluntary, non-coercive social change
* Spiral history: crisis → integration → stillness
* Fusion of science & mysticism (quantum consciousness + perennial wisdom)
* AI-powered low scarcity leading toward post-scarcity leisure

---

**History:**

* **Near-Future Crisis (2028 – 2036):** political unrest and AI job shocks; Sajan builds SP-0 after his daughter's death; first Resting Place opens in Minneapolis; Shangri-La demo synchronises thousands.
* **Transitional Era (2037 – 2060):** The Unravelling fractures old systems; Lorai returns from Mountain Sanctuary; Courier Guild forms; Pebble proliferates; Oneness Summit enshrines Right of Reflection & launches the Memory Loom.
* **Luminous Presence (2061 → + 250 yrs):** technology recedes into seamless ambience; StillPoint evolves to SP-Ω; Commons and Federations co-govern via Memory Loom; daily life centres on shared silence, luminous pathways, and intrinsic wellbeing.
